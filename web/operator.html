Voice & guardrails

how the brand speaks — and what it never says
Always
  • Name the exact corridor — Kokapet, Narsingi, Financial District.
  • Explain landlord-share plainly; treat safety as the buyer's first question.
  • Use price ranges and "verify with the Neopolis team".
  • Speak to NRI buyers on legal comfort and remote purchase.
Never
  • Invent exact prices, legal guarantees, or project claims.
  • Credit a "Reputed developer" or any unnamed builder.
  • Confuse the firm with "Neopolis", the HMDA layout in Kokapet.
  • AI clichés — "delve", "landscape", "game-changer".

Fact rules

hard constraints enforced on every generation
Landlord-share, not builder stock. Flats come to buyers as the landowner's share of a joint development.
West Hyderabad only. Kokapet, Narsingi, Financial District, Manchirevula, Tellapur, Kollur.
If no developer is truly named, print no developer credit — never by Reputed developer.
No fabricated prices. Ranges only, always paired with a verify-with-team line.

The whole product · in one page

One brain per brand. Agents do the work. A human always says yes.

Marketing Brain gives each business its own AI marketing team — a shared source of truth, specialist agents that produce real content, and a human approval gate so nothing goes out that the brand wouldn't say itself. Below is every agent and everything it does, and how the pieces fit.

Company Brain→ Orchestrator→ Specialist agents→ Human approval→ Publish→ Learn

How it works

Six stages, every time. The brand's knowledge goes in one end; approved, on-brand marketing comes out the other — and what worked feeds back in.

1

Company Brain

Each brand's identity, voice, fact rules and visual lock — the single source of truth every agent reads first.

2

Orchestrator plans

Turns a goal ("this week's posts") into a plan: which corridor, which format, which agent, and whether it needs approval.

3

Agents produce

Specialist agents write the copy and render the brand-locked creative, each returning a structured, checkable result.

4

You approve

Every creative arrives with a recommendation, the evidence, expected outcome and risk. You approve, request changes, or reject.

5

Publish & learn

Approved work ships to the channel, then results flow back so the brain gets better at what to make next.
