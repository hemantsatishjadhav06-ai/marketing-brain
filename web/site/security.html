Security & control

Built so that speed never outruns control.

An AI system that can publish on your behalf needs stronger guarantees than one that only writes drafts. These are the controls Marketing Brain is built around — and the claims we deliberately do not make.

Human in the loop, by structure

Approval is a record in the system — with a recommendation, evidence, reason, expected outcome, risk and an append-only trail of who decided what. Code paths that publish or proceed check for an approved record of the current version. Regenerate the asset, and the approval is invalidated.

Approval gates on high-impact actions

These never run without a person, in any execution mode:

  • Publishing a post to any channel
  • Spending money on ads
  • Starting a new campaign
  • Changing brand identity or brand rules
  • Sending a reply from the inbox

Workspace and tenant isolation

Each company is its own workspace with owner, admin and client roles and invite-based access. Brand data, memory, approvals and connected accounts belong to that workspace. Clients see and review their own brand only.

  • Self-serve multi-company workspaces
  • Role-based access: owner, admin, client
  • Invites, not shared passwords

Your channels, your token

Publishing to Instagram and Facebook uses a Meta token you connect yourself. It is used only for the accounts you connected and only for actions you approved. You can revoke it from Meta at any time and publishing stops immediately.

Source-first grounding

Facts come from your sources, or they are flagged.

Prices, dates, specifications, addresses, registration numbers and contact details are treated as facts, not prose. A fact is only used when it can be traced to a verified company source, and every use carries that source and a confidence level.

  • Facts are injected deterministically from approved sources — never guessed by a language model
  • Each factual claim shows its evidence and confidence in the approval card
  • Insufficient evidence produces a flag and a request, not an invented figure

The no-fabrication policy

  1. 1The brain may not state a number, date, or specification it cannot trace to a company source.
  2. 2When it cannot, it marks the claim as unverified and tells you what source would resolve it.
  3. 3Corrections you make in review become memory. The same mistake is not repeated.
  4. 4Rules you set outrank everything else, including the brain's own learnings.

Plain speaking

What we claim, and what we do not.

Marketing tools are prone to overclaiming. We would rather set expectations you can hold us to.

What we do claim

  • Nothing is published, spent, launched or rebranded without a human approval.
  • Factual claims in generated content are grounded in your sources or flagged as unverified.
  • Creatives use your real logo and brand palette, not a look-alike.
  • Corrections persist in brand memory across every specialist.
  • Each workspace's data, memory and connected accounts are its own.
  • The source code is public on GitHub, so the claims above can be checked.

What we do not claim

  • No guaranteed rankings in Google, or in AI assistants and AI search. No one can promise that honestly.
  • No guaranteed leads, revenue, engagement or growth figures.
  • No "trusted by N companies" and no customer logos — we have not published any, and will not invent them.
  • No independent security certification (SOC 2, ISO 27001) at this stage.
  • No fully autonomous marketing. High-impact actions always wait for you, and we think that is the point.
  • No claim that a "Coming" module is available today — see the module status.

Security work is ongoing and visible in the repository. If you find a problem, open an issue on GitHub or contact the workspace owner who invited you.
