Your first hour

You have four jobs. The specialists have the rest.

There are many screens in the app. You can ignore almost all of them. Set the brand up once, then live on one screen — Approvals — and say yes or ask for a change.

  1. 1
    Add the brand.
    Name and website. Then, once, under Settings → Brand kit: logo, colours, voice and the rules the brain must never break.
  2. 2
    Connect your channels.
    Connect Instagram and Facebook with your own Meta token. It is used only for actions you approve.
  3. 3
    Pick an execution mode.
    AI Assist is the sensible default: the brain prepares, you approve. Switch to Autopilot per brand when you trust the routine.
  4. 4
    Live in Approvals.
    Approve, edit or request changes. Every correction becomes memory. Ask "why?" whenever you want the evidence.

Need access?

Workspaces are self-serve. If your company already has one, ask its owner or an admin for an invite — access is by invitation, not by shared login. If you are setting one up, sign up in the live app and you become the owner.
