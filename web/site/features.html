Features

Sixteen modules, organised around the loop — not around agents.

The 36+ specialists work behind these screens. What you see is the marketing workflow: know, think, create, launch, convert, measure, learn, and the controls that sit across all of it.

Know

Build the source of truth.

Available now

Company Brain

The brand's profile, kit and memory: what the company is, how it speaks, what it never says.

  • Brand profile and brand kit — logo, palette, voice
  • Rules, corrections and learnings, in that order of authority
  • Applied to every specialist automatically

Document library with retrieval over uploaded files is on the roadmap.

Available now

Research, competitors & trends

Continuous scanning of competitors and market trends, summarised into things you can act on.

  • Competitor activity summaries
  • Trend signals relevant to the category
  • Feeds the Think step with evidence
Coming

Ask Brain

Ask the company brain a question in plain language and get an answer with its sources attached.

  • Answers grounded in verified company facts
  • Shows the evidence behind each answer
  • Flags when it does not know

Planned. Not yet available in the live app.

Think

Turn knowledge into a plan.

Available now

Command Center

The dashboard: what needs a decision, what is scheduled, what changed since you last looked.

  • Approvals count and queue at a glance
  • Brand overview across the workspace
  • Jump straight into the work that needs you
Available now

Calendar

A content calendar built from approved ideas, balanced across formats and themes.

  • Generated from ideas and brand rules
  • Editable and re-plannable
  • Drives scheduling in Publishing

Create

Make it, on brand, every time.

Available now

Content Studio

Ideas, captions, copy, blog posts and emails written in the brand's voice.

  • Content ideas with rationale
  • Captions and copy variants
  • Blog and email drafts
Available now

Creative Studio

Brand-locked visuals: the real logo composited, the brand palette applied, 4:5 by default.

  • Logo composited onto every render
  • Palette locked to the brand kit
  • Reel studio for short-form video

Launch

Put approved work into the world.

Available now

Publishing

Publish approved posts to Instagram and Facebook through the customer's own Meta token.

  • Instagram and Facebook, live today
  • Uses your token — revoke it any time
  • Every publish sits behind an approval

Additional channels are planned.

Coming

Paid Growth

Campaign proposals with budgets, audiences and creative, prepared for approval.

  • Meta and Google campaign drafts
  • Spend always gated by approval
  • Attribution back to leads

Planned. Ad spend will never run without an explicit human approval.

Coming

SEO & AI Search

Content structured for search engines and AI assistants, grounded in company sources.

  • Keyword and topic coverage
  • Structured, source-backed pages
  • Recommendations, not guarantees

Planned. We do not promise rankings in search engines or AI answers — nobody can.

Convert

Turn attention into conversations.

Available now

Engagement Inbox

Comments and messages from connected channels in one place, with replies drafted for you.

  • Unified inbox across connected accounts
  • AI-drafted replies in the brand voice
  • A human reviews and sends — always
Coming

Leads

Capture, qualify and follow up leads from conversations and campaigns.

  • Lead records tied to source conversation
  • Qualification with evidence
  • Handover to your sales process

Planned alongside the CRM module.

Measure

Compare results to the promise.

Partial

Analytics

Performance against what was expected when the work was approved.

  • Expected outcome recorded on every approval
  • Results viewable per post and per brand
  • Automated pulls from channels: in progress

Partial: manual review works today; scheduled analytics pulls are being built.

Learn

Make the next loop smarter.

Partial

Learning

Corrections and outcomes written back to brand memory so no instruction is given twice.

  • Rules, corrections and learnings, live today
  • Applied across every specialist
  • Closed publish → metrics → learning loop: in progress

Partial: memory is live; automatic learning from performance data follows analytics.

Across the loop

Control that sits over every step.

Available now

Approvals

One queue for everything that needs a decision, each item carrying its reason and evidence.

  • Approve, edit, or request changes
  • Recommendation, evidence, reason, expected outcome, risk
  • Regenerating an asset invalidates its approval
Available now

Automations

Autopilot for predefined low-risk actions, switched on per brand.

  • Per-brand on/off
  • Low-risk actions only
  • High-impact actions always stop at the gate

Statuses are updated as the product changes.

If a module says "Available now", it has been exercised in the live app. If it says "Coming", it has not — and we will not describe it as if it had.
