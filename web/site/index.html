The loop

Seven steps. One continuous cycle. Every step leaves evidence behind.

Most marketing tools do one step and stop. Marketing Brain runs the whole cycle and feeds what it learns back into the next one — with a person able to step in at any point.

  1. KNOWVerified company facts, brand rules, competitors and trends become the source of truth.
  2. THINKStrategy and content ideas are proposed with the reasoning and evidence behind them.
  3. CREATEOn-brand creatives, captions, blogs and emails are drafted with your real logo and palette.
  4. LAUNCHApproved work is scheduled and published to your connected channels.
  5. CONVERTComments and messages land in one inbox; replies are drafted for a human to send.
  6. MEASUREResults are gathered against what was promised when the work was approved.
  7. LEARNCorrections and outcomes are written back to brand memory so the next loop starts smarter.

Internally the loop runs as twelve stages: Research → Understand → Strategize → Create → Approve → Publish → Promote → Engage → Capture leads → Measure → Learn → Improve. You see workflows, not agents.

Execution modes

You decide how much the brain does on its own.

Three modes, set per brand. Move between them whenever you like. The mode changes how much preparation happens without you — never who gets the final say on high-impact actions.

Mode 1

Manual

You control every step.

The brain researches, suggests and drafts only when asked. Nothing moves forward until you move it.

  • Best for onboarding a new brand
  • Every action is a deliberate click
Mode 2

AI Assist

AI prepares, a human approves.

Ideas, calendars, creatives and replies arrive ready for review. You approve, edit, or send them back with a correction the brain remembers.

  • The default for most teams
  • Live on one screen: Approvals
Mode 3

Autopilot

AI executes predefined low-risk actions.

Routine, low-risk work — research refreshes, idea generation, draft preparation — runs on a schedule per brand. High-impact actions still stop at the gate.

  • Scoped per brand, switchable any time
  • Never publishes or spends without approval

Always requires human approval, in every mode. These actions are gated by design, not by setting.

  • Publishing a post
  • Spending on ads
  • Starting a new campaign
  • Changing brand identity
  • Sending a reply from the inbox

Why it is different

Not another content generator. An operating system with a memory and a conscience.

Source-first AI

Every factual claim — a price, a specification, a location, a date — is grounded in a verified company source. Each one carries its evidence and a confidence level. When the evidence is not good enough, the brain says so and asks, instead of inventing a number that sounds right.

Human control, structurally

Approval is not a toggle you can forget about. It is an object in the system: a recommendation, its evidence, the reason, the expected outcome and the risk — with a record of who decided what. Publishing, ad spend, new campaigns and brand changes cannot proceed without one.

Persistent brand memory

Rules outrank corrections, corrections outrank learnings. Tell the brain once that you never use exclamation marks, or that a floor plan is not to be shown, and that instruction applies to every specialist from then on. A correction is never given twice.

Explore the learning modules

  1. RulesSet by you. Non-negotiable. "Never quote a price without a source."
  2. CorrectionsGiven once in review. "Use 'residences', not 'flats'."
  3. LearningsObserved from results. "Carousel posts outperform on this account."
