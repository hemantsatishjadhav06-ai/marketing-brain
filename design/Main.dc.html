Marketing Brain · Getting started

You have four jobs.
The agents have the rest.

There are a lot of screens in here. You can ignore almost all of them. Set the brand up once, then live on one screen — Approvals — and say yes or ask for a change.

Everything below happens in the order it is written. Nothing else is required to ship your first post.

1

Add the brand

Sidebar → + New Brand. Give it a name and the website. That is the whole form.

Then, once, under Settings → Brand kit:
Upload your logo. It gets placed on every creative at full fidelity — not redrawn.
Set your brand colours. Nothing outside them will ever be used.
2

Leave it on Auto

Auto is the default and the way this is meant to run. The agents research, write, art-direct and render on their own, then stop and wait for you.

Auto — recommended
Agents do the work. You only approve.
Manual
Nothing generates by itself. You drive every step. Most people never need this.
3

Work the Approvals screen

This is the screen you live on. Everything finished lands here, oldest first, with the image and the caption. Two buttons, and that is the entire daily job.

Approve
Good to go. It is scheduled — and the agents learn that this angle works.
Change this…
Type what should be different. It is redone — and remembered, so you never give that note twice.
4

Correct it once, not every week

Every approval and every change you ask for becomes brand memory. If there is something the agents should never do, say it once under Settings → 🧠 Memory and it holds from then on.

Rules people actually set
rule Never use the word “luxury” more than once in a caption.
rule No festive or greeting posts for this brand.
correction Written for you whenever you use “Change this…”.

That is the whole loop. Add the brand, leave it on Auto, approve or ask for a change, and correct it once. Every other screen in the sidebar is optional depth you can open when you actually want it.
